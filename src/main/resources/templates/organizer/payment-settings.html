Organizer payment settings

Direct UPI ID payments

Set the UPI ID that receives direct payments for your events. Attendees will submit a UPI transaction reference, and you must verify the actual credit in your bank/UPI app before issuing tickets.

Example format: name@bank. Do not enter a UPI PIN or bank password here.

Razorpay Route (optional)

Enter the Razorpay Route linked account ID created for your organizer account. This does not itself complete onboarding.

Status:

Never enter API secrets or UPI PINs here. Direct UPI payments remain pending until you independently verify the incoming payment.
